## Technical & Regulatory Policy Content

Blast Furnace Slag & Metallurgy Quality Guidelines for Structural Steel Production: 1. Slag Basicity Index: Binary Basicity B2 = %CaO / %SiO2 must be strictly maintained between 1.15 and 1.25 for optimal desulphurisation and alkali removal. 2. Hot Metal Temperature at tap hole is maintained at 1,480°C to 1,510°C. Silicon (Si) content target is 0.45% to 0.65%. 3. IS 2062 Structural Steel Grade E250 / E350: Yield Strength minimum 250 MPa / 350 MPa, Tensile Strength 410-530 MPa, Minimum Elongation 23%, and Charpy V-notch impact test at 0°C / -20°C.
