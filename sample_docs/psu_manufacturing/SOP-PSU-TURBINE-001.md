## Technical & Regulatory Policy Content

Supercritical & Subcritical Steam Turbine (TG-501) Engineering Specifications: 1. Shaft Vibration Limits as per ISO 7919-2 / ISO 10816: Normal steady operation < 45 micrometers peak-to-peak. Alarm at 80 micrometers. Trip setpoint at 120 micrometers. 2. Bearing Metal Temperature: Normal operating temperature is 65°C to 80°C. Alarm threshold is 95°C; emergency auto-trip threshold is 105°C. 3. Turbine Barring Gear (Turning Gear): Mandatory engagement at 120-150 RPM during cooldown until HP casing inner metal temperature drops below 150°C to prevent rotor bowing. 4. Heat Rate: Design Gross Turbine Heat Rate (GTHR) for 660MW Supercritical unit is 1,840 kcal/kWh at 247 kg/cm² main steam pressure and 565°C / 593°C reheat temperature.
