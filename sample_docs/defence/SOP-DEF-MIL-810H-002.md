## Technical & Regulatory Policy Content

MIL-STD-810H Environmental Testing Standards for Tactical Defence Electronics & Subsystems: 1. Method 501.7 (High Temperature): Operating temperature qualification between +55°C and +71°C continuous for 48 hours; Storage test at +85°C. 2. Method 502.7 (Low Temperature): Extreme Cold operational testing at -40°C (High Altitude Leh/Ladakh baseline) and -50°C storage. 3. Method 509.7 (Salt Fog Corrosion): 5% NaCl saline atmosphere exposure for 48 hours followed by 48 hours drying, repeated for 4 cycles to qualify coastal/naval installations. 4. Method 514.8 (Random Vibration): Tactical wheeled and tracked vehicle vibration spectral density curve from 10 Hz to 2,000 Hz at 5.4 Grms for 6 hours per axis.
