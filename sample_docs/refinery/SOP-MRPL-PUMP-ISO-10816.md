## Technical & Regulatory Policy Content

Vibration Velocity Severity criteria as per ISO 10816-3 (Rigid Foundation, Group 1/2 Industrial Machines): 1. Zone A (Newly Commissioned / Good): Overall vibration RMS velocity < 2.3 mm/s. 2. Zone B (Acceptable for Long-term Operation): 2.3 mm/s to 4.5 mm/s RMS. 3. Zone C (Warning / Alert Limit): 4.5 mm/s to 7.1 mm/s RMS — schedule vibration spectral analysis (1X unbalance, 2X misalignment, blade pass). 4. Zone D (Danger / Immediate Shutdown Trip): > 7.1 mm/s RMS. 5. Bearing Housing Temperature: Maximum allowable bearing metal temp is 85°C (185°F). Alarm set at 75°C.
