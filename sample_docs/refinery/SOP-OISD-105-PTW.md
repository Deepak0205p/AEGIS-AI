## Technical & Regulatory Policy Content

OISD-STD-105 specifies strict regulatory guidelines for Permit to Work (PTW) across Indian hydrocarbon installations. 1. Hot Work Permit is mandatory for any open-flame, welding, cutting, grinding, or spark-generating activity within battery limits. 2. Gas Testing Protocol: Mandatory multi-gas testing must verify: Oxygen between 19.5% and 23.5% v/v, Combustible gas/vapor (LEL) = 0.0%, and toxic gas concentration (H2S < 10 ppm, Benzene < 0.02 ppm, CO < 25 ppm) prior to permit issuance. 3. Hot Work permits remain valid for a maximum of 8 hours (single shift) and require mandatory re-validation by Shift In-Charge. 4. Fire watch with charged 10 kg DCP fire extinguisher and pressurized fire water hose is mandatory within 15 meters.
